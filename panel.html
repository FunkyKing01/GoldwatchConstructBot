Deposit and Retrieval

People type /deposit in Discord, pick an item from this list and enter a quantity. Moderators type /retrieve the same way to take items out, which subtracts from the totals.

Who can use /retrieve is set under Moderators below.

Moderators

Moderators can use /retrieve (take items out) and /records (see every item and its total, shown only to them). People who can manage the server are always moderators.
